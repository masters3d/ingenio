## Understanding the Confusion: "Autonomy might align with Search"

### The Concern

A surface-level interpretation suggests:
- **Autonomy** = freedom to explore → could relate to **Search**
- **Mastery** = executing with skill → could relate to **Drive**

This interpretation makes sense at first glance: "autonomy is where you would know how to search."

### Why This is Backwards

This confuses the **operational cycle phases**. The correct interpretation follows the temporal sequence:

**Temporal Sequence:**
```
1. PROSPECTIVE (Search/Mastery)    → "What should I aim for?"
2. ACTUATION (Drive/Autonomy)      → "How do I execute with control?"
3. RETROSPECTIVE (Renew/Purpose)   → "Was this the right thing?"
```

### The Correct Mapping

#### 1. Search ↔ Mastery (PROSPECTIVE/KNOWING)

**Cycle Phase**: Prospective — understanding BEFORE acting

**Search asks**: "What does *better* look like?"
**Mastery seeks**: The urge to get better at things that matter

**Why this alignment**:
- Before you can act, you must first KNOW what to aim for
- Search is the prospective discovery of what "better" means
- Mastery is the internal drive to grow in skills
- This is the KNOWING phase — understanding what success looks like

**Example**:
> "I need to master distributed systems" (Search for what matters) → Then you learn it (Actuation) → Then you verify it was worth learning (Retrospective)

**NOT**:
> "I have autonomy to explore distributed systems" — This is about permission to act, not about defining what "better" means

#### 2. Drive ↔ Autonomy (ACTUATION/ACTING)

**Cycle Phase**: Actuation — executing with control

**Drive asks**: "What can I control?"
**Autonomy desires**: The freedom to direct your own work and own outcomes

**Why this alignment**:
- Once you know what to pursue (Search/Mastery), you need the AUTHORITY to pursue it
- Drive is about degrees of freedom in action
- Autonomy is about ownership and decision-making power
- This is the ACTING phase — executing with control over how you work

**Example**:
> "I have autonomy to choose JWT vs session tokens" (Drive to act with authority) → Based on my understanding of auth patterns (Search/Mastery)

**NOT**:
> "I search for what authentication approach to use" — This is about learning options (Prospective), not about permission to decide (Actuation)

#### 3. Renew ↔ Purpose (RETROSPECTIVE/IMPROVING)

**Cycle Phase**: Retrospective — verifying alignment

**Renew asks**: "Am I optimizing for the right thing?"
**Purpose connects**: Work to meaningful goals beyond personal gain

**Why this alignment**:
- After acting (Drive/Autonomy), you verify the objective function was correct
- Renew is about checking if you're still aligned with what matters
- Purpose is about meaningful work that serves larger goals
- This is the IMPROVING phase — verifying you're optimizing for the right thing

**Example**:
> "Is this technical work still serving our users' needs?" (Renew purpose alignment) → After implementing with autonomy (Actuation) → Based on mastery of the domain (Prospective)

## Why "Autonomy enables Search" is True but Different

### The User's Intuition is Correct About a Different Relationship

**True statement**: Autonomy enables effective Search
**But this is an ENABLER relationship, not a MAPPING relationship**

**Enabler Pattern**:
```
Drive (Autonomy) enables effective Search (Mastery)
   ↓
"I have the freedom to explore" (Autonomy)
   ↓
Enables me to discover what to learn (Search/Mastery)
```

**This is a CROSS-PILLAR INTERACTION, not a same-pillar mapping**:
- Drive/Autonomy in the ACTUATION phase enables better Search/Mastery in the next PROSPECTIVE phase
- The operational cycle is a loop: Retrospective feeds back to Prospective

**Example**:
> An engineer with high autonomy (Drive) can explore different approaches, which enhances their ability to identify what skills matter most (Search/Mastery)

But this doesn't mean Autonomy IS Search. It means Autonomy ENABLES better Search in the next cycle iteration.
